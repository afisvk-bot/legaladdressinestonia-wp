## content_blocks_0_content
For a Ukrainian entrepreneur, an Estonian OÜ is a European company that can be managed from Ukraine: contracts with EU clients, payment services that do not work with Ukrainian entities, and corporate income tax only when profit is paid out. Ukraine is among the top five countries by number of Estonian e-residents, and its citizens can use a dedicated programme that compensates state fees.

But for a resident of Ukraine, an Estonian company lives in two tax systems at once. In Estonia: 22/78 tax on profit distributions, board member remuneration, VAT. In Ukraine: a notice and an annual report on a controlled foreign company. And if your team works in Ukraine as FOPs (Ukrainian sole proprietors), the Estonian company also gets a VAT obligation on services from abroad, something almost nobody warns about.
<blockquote>We register OÜs for Ukrainian entrepreneurs, provide a legal address and a contact person, keep the books and prepare financial statements so that they can be attached to the CFC report in Ukraine.</blockquote>

## content_blocks_8_title_right
Key insight: a team in Ukraine creates VAT in Estonia
## content_blocks_8_text_right
The most common model: the Estonian OÜ sells to EU clients, while developers and designers in Ukraine invoice it as FOPs. Almost nobody writes about the tax consequence of this model.

Services that an Estonian company receives from a supplier outside the EU are subject to Estonian VAT under the reverse charge rule. If the OÜ is VAT-registered, it charges 24% and immediately deducts it, so the net result is zero. If the OÜ is not registered, it must register as a limited VAT payer and pay 24% on these services with no right to deduct. There is no threshold.

<b>Practical conclusion:</b> for the "clients in the EU, team in Ukraine" model, the VAT registration question is decided by calculation, not by default. Sometimes a standard registration is more advantageous because the tax on the team's services becomes neutral. We calculate both options on your figures before the first payment to contractors.
## content_blocks_9_title_left
When an Estonian company does not fit and when it is not enough
## content_blocks_9_text_left
An honest review saves more than the registration.
<ul>
<li><b>You live in Estonia under temporary protection.</b> You do not need e-Residency and will not be issued it: the company can be set up with an Estonian ID card. Your tax residency may already be Estonian, in which case Ukrainian CFC rules may not apply to you.</li>
<li><b>You live in Poland or Germany.</b> Tax residency is determined by the country where you live, and the Estonian company falls under its CFC rules. Residency comes first, structure second.</li>
<li><b>All your clients are in Ukraine.</b> An Estonian company will add reporting but no advantages: contracts with Ukrainian clients are easier through a FOP or a Ukrainian LLC.</li>
<li><b>You want an Estonian VAT number "for credibility".</b> Without an economic link to Estonia, EMTA may refuse. B2B clients in the EU are invoiced under reverse charge even without an Estonian VAT number.</li>
<li><b>You need to take the money out immediately.</b> The Estonian model pays off when profit stays in the company. If everything is paid out as dividends, the 22/78 tax in Estonia plus the dividend tax in Ukraine costs more than a FOP.</li>
</ul>

<h2><b>How a Ukrainian can open and run a company in Estonia: practice</b></h2>
<h3><b>e-Residency from Ukraine</b></h3>
The application is submitted online in the Police and Border Guard Board environment. You need a passport, a photo and a description of why you need Estonian e-services. The state fee is €150, rising to €165 from 1 January 2027. The pick-up point is chosen in the form: the Estonian Embassy in Kyiv or the mobile pick-up point in Lviv coordinated by the embassy in Kyiv. For men of conscription age during martial law, this is often the only realistic option. The card is kept at the pick-up point for 6 months. More in our article <a href="https://staging.dragonslots-gr.gr/blog-ru/kak-poluchit-e-residency-2026/">how to obtain e-Residency in 2026</a>.

<h3><b>Taxes in Estonia</b></h3>
As long as profit stays in the company, there is no corporate income tax. On distribution, the company pays 22/78. A board member's remuneration is subject in Estonia to 22% income tax and 33% social tax, even if you live in Ukraine. More in our article <a href="https://staging.dragonslots-gr.gr/blog-ru/korporativnyj-nalog-estoniya-2026/">Estonian corporate tax in 2026</a>.

For owners resident in Ukraine, banks and payment institutions carry out enhanced due diligence: source of funds, clients, sanctions risks. <h3><b>Place of management</b></h3>
If all decisions are made in Ukraine, the Ukrainian side may see a permanent establishment in the company's activities. Contracts, board resolutions and the real role of the Estonian infrastructure must reflect the picture honestly: this is part of the set-up, not a formality.

## content_blocks_12_faq_items_2_question

## content_blocks_12_faq_items_2_answer
Yes, if you are a Ukrainian tax resident and control the company. The notice is due within 60 days of setting up the company and the report every year, even with zero profit.
## content_blocks_12_faq_items_3_question
Do I pay tax in Ukraine on the Estonian company's profit?
## content_blocks_12_faq_items_3_answer
If the total income of all your CFCs does not exceed €2 million a year, CFC profit is exempt from tax, but the report must still be filed. Dividends received personally are taxed at 9% personal income tax plus 5% military levy.
## content_blocks_12_faq_items_4_question
Can I pay my team in Ukraine through FOPs?
## content_blocks_12_faq_items_4_answer
Yes, but services from a supplier outside the EU are subject to Estonian VAT under the reverse charge. Without a standard VAT registration, the company pays 24% on these services with no deduction.
## content_blocks_12_faq_items_5_question
Can I close my FOP after opening an OÜ?
## content_blocks_12_faq_items_5_answer
You can, but you do not have to. Many keep their FOP for Ukrainian clients and use the OÜ for European ones.
## content_blocks_12_faq_items_6_question

## content_blocks_12_faq_items_6_answer
The decision always lies with the bank, and the checks are enhanced. The chances are higher with contracts, a clear model and a website; many companies work through payment institutions.
## content_blocks_12_faq_items_7_question

## content_blocks_12_faq_items_7_answer
You will not be issued e-Residency: the company is set up with an Estonian ID card. Check your tax residency, because it determines whether Ukrainian CFC rules apply.
