## content_blocks_0_content
As soon as an Estonian company becomes VAT-registered, it gets a monthly rhythm: by the 20th, a KMD return with the KMD INF annex, and for sales to businesses in the EU, a VD report as well. The return is filed every month, even if there was no turnover.

The difficulty is not the form itself. The difficulty is that every line of the KMD is a decision: which rate, where the place of supply is, whether the client's VAT number is valid, whether input VAT on this expense can be deducted. And the tax authority automatically matches KMD INF data with your counterparties' returns, so a discrepancy turns into an enquiry before you even know about it.
<blockquote>We prepare and file the KMD, KMD INF and VD, check rates and place of supply for every transaction, reconcile data with counterparties and banks, correct past periods and prepare your bookkeeping for the new data-based KMD that starts in 2027.</blockquote>

## content_blocks_7_title_right
Key insight: VAT breaks in three places, and none of them shows up on the form
## content_blocks_7_text_right
The KMD form is simple: it has a few dozen lines. Errors arise earlier, when a transaction is classified.
<ul>
<li><b>Purchases from foreign suppliers.</b> Google and Meta ads, hosting, SaaS subscriptions, services from contractors in other countries: these are services received on which the company itself charges VAT in Estonia. A company without a standard registration becomes a limited VAT payer for this, with no threshold. This is the most common finding in a review.</li>
<li><b>Reverse charge without checking the number.</b> Invoicing an EU B2B client without VAT is only allowed if their number is valid in VIES on the supply date. An invalid number means 24% on your side, and you usually find out during an audit.</li>
<li><b>The wrong period.</b> A prepayment creates turnover in the month the money is received, a credit note is reported in the month it is issued, and an error is corrected in the return for the period in which it was made. Moving it "to the current month" is a common cause of discrepancies with counterparties.</li>
</ul>
<b>Practical conclusion:</b> a reliable KMD starts not with filling in the form but with a rule for each flow of transactions: which rate, which place of supply, which document. We set these rules up front, and from then on each month closes without guesswork.
## content_blocks_8_title_left
When you do not need a KMD and when it is not enough
## content_blocks_8_text_left
An honest review saves more than the service itself.
<ul>
<li><b>No obligation to register.</b> If Estonian taxable supplies are below €40,000 and your sales are B2B in the EU under reverse charge or exports of services, standard VAT registration may not be needed. Sometimes it is not worth it: you will have to file a KMD every month with almost nothing to deduct.</li>
<li><b>Voluntary registration without a link to Estonia.</b> Since August 2025, EMTA registers voluntarily only where there is an economic link to Estonia. Without one, registration may be refused and an existing number revoked.</li>
<li><b>Sales to consumers in the EU.</b> This is not the KMD but OSS, a separate quarterly return. More on <a href="{L:2346}">OSS and IOSS registration</a>.</li>
<li><b>A warehouse in another EU country.</b> Storing goods abroad creates an obligation to register for VAT locally. The Estonian KMD does not replace it.</li>
<li><b>A small business in several EU countries.</b> Since 2025, the small enterprise exemption scheme applies with EU turnover up to €100,000 and Estonian turnover up to €40,000. It is sometimes more advantageous than registration; this needs to be calculated.</li>
</ul>

<h2><b>What VAT reporting of an Estonian company includes</b></h2>
Every month a VAT payer files up to three documents, and they check one another.

<td>Every month, including zero months</td>
</tr>
<tr>

<td>Data of issued and received invoices for Estonian transactions with VAT payers</td>
<td>Every month together with the KMD</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>VD</td>
<td>Supplies of goods and services to B2B clients in other EU countries by client number</td>

The KMD INF of your sales is the KMD INF of your clients' purchases. EMTA matches them automatically and shows discrepancies in the tax behaviour rating. If a difference with a counterparty's data appears there, the cause is first clarified with the counterparty, not with the tax authority.

<h3><b>Restrictions on input VAT deduction</b></h3>
Not all input VAT is deductible. Expenses not related to taxable turnover give no right to deduction. For passenger cars, deduction is restricted if the car is not used solely for business. Entertainment and employee expenses are checked separately. With both taxable and exempt turnover, the deduction is calculated pro rata.
<h3><b>Corrections and credit notes</b></h3>
If a return contains an error, for example turnover was not declared, a new KMD is filed for the same period with corrected data. A credit note or invoice cancellation issued after the return was filed is reported by the seller and buyer in the period it is issued. The difference between an "error" and a "subsequent change" is a common source of discrepancies.
<h3><b>Refund of overpayment</b></h3>
If input VAT exceeds output VAT, the difference is refunded. EMTA may extend the refund period for a review. A refund cannot be claimed while the company has outstanding returns for the current and two previous years, so order in past periods directly affects your money.
<h3><b>Data-based KMD from 2027</b></h3>
EMTA is moving VAT reporting to the same principle as the TSD from October 2026: the company transfers transaction data from its accounting software and the return is generated from it. Under the announced plan, the KMD and VD merge into one return, the KMD INF threshold is abolished, and CSV uploads will need a new file version. Testing for software developers opens on 1 January 2027, and filing under the new scheme starts in spring 2027. This prepares for the EU reform with mandatory e-invoicing and near real-time data reporting from 1 July 2030.

<b>What this means for you:</b> bookkeeping with transactions tagged by type and counterparty already now means a KMD without rework in 2027.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_0_question
When is the KMD filed?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_0_answer
Monthly, by the 20th of the month following the reporting month, together with the KMD INF annex. The amount payable is transferred to the EMTA prepayment account by the same deadline.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_1_question
Do I need to file a KMD if there was no turnover?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_1_answer
Yes. A registered VAT payer files a KMD every month, including a zero return.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_2_question
What is the VAT rate in Estonia in 2026?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_2_answer
The standard rate is 24% from 1 July 2025. Reduced rates are 13% for accommodation and 9% for books, press and medicines. 0% applies to exports and supplies to the EU.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_3_question
What is the KMD INF?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_3_answer
An annex to the KMD with data of issued and received invoices for Estonian transactions with VAT payers. EMTA matches this data with your counterparties' returns.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_4_question
When is a VD report needed?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_4_answer
For months with supplies of goods or services to B2B clients in other EU countries. The deadline is the same, the 20th.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_5_question
What is limited VAT liability?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_5_answer
A registration for companies without standard VAT registration that receive services or goods from abroad. The company charges VAT on such purchases itself, there is no threshold, and this tax cannot be deducted.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_6_question
How do I correct an error in a filed KMD?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_6_answer
File a corrected KMD for the period in which the error was made. Credit notes issued later are reported in the period they are issued.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_7_question
What is the interest for late payment?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_7_answer
0.06% per day on the unpaid amount, or 21.9% per year, from the first day after the deadline. With an instalment plan the rate can be reduced by up to half.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_8_question
What will change in the KMD in 2027?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_8_answer
The KMD will be filed as data from accounting software, the KMD and VD will merge into one return, and the KMD INF threshold will be abolished.
