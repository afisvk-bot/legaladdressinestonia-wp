## content_blocks_0_content
A board member (juhatuse liige) is the legal representative of an Estonian OÜ: they sign contracts, file reports and are personally liable for deadlines. That is why changing the board is not an internal formality but a change that must be entered in the Commercial Register. Until the entry is made, banks, the tax authority and counterparties still see the previous board running the company.

The entry itself is made online in one or two business days. The complications are around it: the new board member cannot sign the application digitally; the outgoing one does not respond; the whole board ends up abroad and the company needs a contact person; the bank still sees the old director; access to e-MTA and the accounting software stays with someone who is no longer responsible for the company.
<blockquote>We prepare the shareholder resolution and the new board member's consent, file the application in the e-Business Register and close everything that follows the entry: e-MTA access, the bank, the contact person and beneficial owner data.</blockquote>

## content_blocks_7_title_right
Key insight: the register entry is only half the job
## content_blocks_7_text_right
Owners consider the board change complete once the new name appears in the register. Problems arise in what the register does not change automatically.
<ul>
<li><b>The bank.</b> Register data is not passed to the bank. Until signatories are updated, payments may be approved by someone no longer responsible for the company, while the new board member has no access to the account.</li>
<li><b>Joint representation.</b> If the company has several board members with joint signing rights, e-MTA rights are not granted automatically: a power of attorney from all of them is needed.</li>
<li><b>The board moves abroad.</b> If the last board member resident in Estonia leaves, the company needs a contact person. Without one, the registrar may start deletion proceedings.</li>
<li><b>The previous director's legacy.</b> An unfiled annual report or a tax debt is now the new board's problem. The new board member is responsible for the company from the moment they take office.</li>
</ul>
<b>Practical takeaway:</b> a board change is a package of the entry, access rights, the bank and a check of past periods. We handle all of it.
## content_blocks_8_title_left
When a board change is not needed and when it is not enough
## content_blocks_8_text_left
<ul>
<li><b>You only need someone to sign contracts.</b> You do not have to change the board for that: a power of attorney or a procura is enough.</li>
<li><b>The company owner changes.</b> A board change does not transfer shares. First comes the <a href="{L:4845}">share transfer</a>, then the board change and UBO update.</li>
<li><b>A board member wants to leave and there is no replacement.</b> A company cannot be left without a board. If the board is vacant, the registrar may warn of compulsory liquidation, so find a replacement in advance.</li>
<li><b>The term of office has expired.</b> If the articles limit the term, the board member must be re-elected and the entry updated when it ends. Otherwise the register shows a board whose term has expired.</li>
<li><b>The whole company is sold.</b> The board change is part of the deal, not a separate procedure: it has to be synchronised with the share transfer and the bank.</li>
</ul>

<h2><b>Who a board member of an OÜ is and what changes when they are replaced</b></h2>
In an OÜ, the management board is the only mandatory governing body. A board member represents the company, signs contracts and reports, and is responsible for bookkeeping and filing the annual report. A board member contract is not an employment contract: employee protection rules do not apply, and the fee is subject to 22% income tax and 33% social tax.

<h2><b>How to change a board member of an OÜ: in practice</b></h2>
<h3><b>Step-by-step process</b></h3>
<ol>
<li><b>Review of the articles.</b> Who appoints the board, how many members, the term of office and the representation rules.</li>
<li><b>Shareholder resolution.</b> On removing the previous and appointing the new board member; adopted at a meeting or without one and signed digitally.</li>
<li><b>Consent of the new board member.</b> Confirmation that they agree to take office and there are no legal obstacles.</li>
<li><b>Application to the register.</b> Filed by a board member via the e-Business Register; without a digital signature, through a notary.</li>
<li><b>Register entry.</b> Usually 1–2 business days; state fee of about €25.</li>
<li><b>After the entry.</b> e-MTA rights, the bank, the contact person, UBO, the contract and remuneration.</li>
</ol>
<h3><b>Signatures: digital or notary</b></h3>
If all signatories have e-Residency, an Estonian ID card or Smart-ID, everything is done online in a day. If the new board member is not in this system, their consent and the application are signed at a notary, and some countries require an apostille. That adds anywhere from a few days to a couple of weeks, so plan the change with a margin.
<h3><b>Resignation of a board member</b></h3>
A board member can resign, and the shareholders can remove them at any time. If the outgoing board member does not take part, the entry is made on the basis of the shareholder resolution. The board member contract and any payments on departure are governed separately.
<h3><b>Board abroad</b></h3>
If all board members are outside Estonia, the company must appoint a contact person: a notary, attorney, auditor, tax representative or licensed corporate service provider. The address of the board abroad is also entered in the register.
<h3><b>Taxes and remuneration</b></h3>
A board member fee is taxed in Estonia at 22% income tax and 33% social tax, without unemployment insurance. The board member is entered in the employment register. Read more in our article on <a href="{L:1037}">taxes for companies in Estonia</a>.
<h3><b>What a new board member should check</b></h3>
Before taking office, check whether the annual reports have been filed, whether there is a tax debt, who has access to the bank and e-MTA, and where the accounting documents are kept. From the entry onwards, you are responsible for all of it.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_0_question
Who appoints a board member of an OÜ?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_0_answer
The company's shareholders by resolution, unless the articles have assigned this to a supervisory board.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_1_question
How much does a board member change cost?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_1_answer
The state fee for changing an OÜ's data in the register is usually €25. Add the cost of preparing documents and, if needed, a notary abroad.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_2_question
How long does the change take?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_2_answer
The entry is usually made within 1–2 business days of a complete filing. If a notary abroad and an apostille are needed, it takes longer.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_3_question
Do I need a notary?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_3_answer
No, if the signatories have a digital signature with e-Residency, an Estonian ID card or Smart-ID. Without one, the documents are certified by a notary.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_4_question
Can a foreigner be a board member of an Estonian company?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_4_answer
Yes. But if the whole board is abroad, the company needs a contact person in Estonia.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_5_question
Do tax authority rights change automatically?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_5_answer
Yes, with sole representation rights, the day after the entry. The bank does not receive register data, so signatories there must be updated separately.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_6_question
How do I remove a board member who does not respond?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_6_answer
The shareholders remove them by resolution, and the entry is made without their signature.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_7_question
Is a new board member liable for past breaches?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_7_answer
For unfiled reports and ongoing obligations, from the moment they take office. That is why it is worth checking the company's condition before the appointment.
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_8_question
Do beneficial owner data need updating when the board changes?
## content_blocks_11_faq_items_8_answer
If only the board changes, usually not. If the owners or control change, yes, within 30 days.
